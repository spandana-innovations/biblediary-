  - key: homily
    title: Homily Tip
    ref: "Matthew 23:23–26"
    audio: null
    body: |
      Some of the astrologists had predicted that the year 2000 would be the end of the world so many people in different countries committed suicide because they were frightened to face the end of the world and the Lord’s second coming. We have reached the year 2024 and again false prophets and soothsayers have raised their heads predicting that the corona pandemic and the climate changes around the world are signs that the world is coming to an end. This is not something new but these kinds of speculations and false teachings were already prevalent among the people of Thessalonian community that the Lord would immediately return and people lived in anxiety and fear. Paul is inviting the community to live a life of holiness by holding to the great traditions and teachings that he has given to them and make the Word part of their life rather than going after these false rumours. Paul would say in Rom 14/8 ‘If we live, we live to the Lord, and if we die, we die to the Lord; so then, whether we live or whether we die, we are the Lord’s’. Therefore Jesus invites the scribes and Pharisees to live a life of holiness by confirming their minds and hearts to God.



      Am I waiting for the signs to happen in the sky to believe in Jesus and live as Jesus expects?



      *(Fr Saji Antony SSP)*
  - key: reflection
    title: Reflection
    ref: null
    audio: null
    body: |
      Today’s Gospel passage presents two ‘woes’ of Jesus. Jesus noticed that the Scribes and the Pharisees were more concerned about the accidentals than the essential things in life. He uses symbols from daily life and condemns them for misleading the people who failed to distinguish between what is important and what is secondary. Among the essentials Jesus mentions justice, mercy and faith, which are far more important than being faithful in giving tithes. Jesus also condemns the Jewish authority for their externalism to the extent that they have forgotten the fact that the essential attribute of religion is the internal disposition of a person. In what way can I capture the heart of religion as far as the essential and internal things are concerned? Saint Monica lived an authentic, holy Christian life and prayed for the internal transformation of her son Augustine.
  - key: saint
    title: Saint of the Day
    saintName: "St Monica - Widow (331-387)"
    ref: "St Monica - Widow (331-387)"
    audio: null
    body: |
      Monica, a Christian, was married at the age of 20 to Patricius, a pagan official at Tagaste (North Africa), whose violent temper and dissolute pagan habits caused her much suffering, making the Christian education of their offspring rather difficult; but after 18 years of married life Patricius, whom she had borne three children, finally embraced the Faith, and died soon after.

      Thenceforth Augustine, her eldest son, was her sole preoccupation, for during his studies at Carthage he had become ensnared by the Manichaean heresy, and kept a woman as mistress for the next 15 years. Monica tried in vain to persuade a visiting Bishop, who had himself passed the Manichaean errors, to lead her son back to the truth; she had to content herself with the bishop’s assurance that “it is impossible that the son of such tears should perish!”

      Monica followed Augustine to Milan, where she befriended the great Bishop, St Ambrose whose eloquence and sanctity helped Augustine to come into the Church at Easter 387.

      “My son, I had but one object in life, and that was to see you a Catholic,” Monica once said to Augustine; “God has done more than I had dared to hope for in that he has taught you to despise all that the world can offer in order to follow him. Bury my body where you will. All I ask is that you remember me always at the altar of God!”

      Monica died in the arms of Augustine in Ostia, the port of Rome, aged 56.
