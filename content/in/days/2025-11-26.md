    ref: "Luke 21:12–19"
    audio: null
    body: |
      Reflection: 1



      Before the end-time comes, persecutions are sure to come, and the followers of Jesus will have to endure it. At such hard times Christians can prove their loyalty to Jesus, and he will empower them to be the final victors. Therefore Jesus assures us, we need not worry at all; he will give us wisdom and words to answer our persecutors, and by his grace we shall endure to the end and save our lives.



      *Fr (Dr) George Kaitholil, SSP*Reflection: 2



      Today’s Gospel gives Jesus’ prophetic warning to the apostles and disciples about the sufferings they will have to bear for their Faith in Him until Jesus’ *Second Coming.* Jesus advises them to bear witness to Him in spite of persecutions, for those persecutions would also encourage the disciples to flee to remote places and to preach the Gospel among the Jews and the Gentiles. Believers, Jesus warns, will be locked up in prisons and brought for trial before kings and governors. Jesus assures them that the Holy Spirit will give them words of defence and witness-bearing. (In the Acts of the Apostles*,* we read how Stephen was given the wisdom to bear splendid witness to Jesus in Jerusalem). Since there will be divisions in families between believers and non-believers, Jesus declares, close relatives will betray their Christian family members to the pagan authorities and cause their martyrdom. But Jesus assures the disciples in today’s Gospel passage that their suffering for Him will be amply rewarded.



      <span class="subhead">Life messages:</span>



      1) Although we may not get a chance to die for the Faith, we are invited to face “dry martyrdom,” a “living death” as outcasts in our contemporary materialistic, secular, liberal, agnostic, and atheistic society.



      2) We are called to bear witness to Christ by loving those who hate us, by showing mercy and compassion to those who hurt and ill-treat us, by forgiving those who continue to offend us, by accepting our sufferings without complaint, and by continuing to keep Jesus’ word in our lives.

    body: |
      In Luke 21:12-19, we hear profound messages that hold great significance. “Do Not Worry” and “Not a hair of your head will perish.” In a world filled with uncertainties and challenges, Jesus reassures us that even in the face of adversity, we need not succumb to worry. He offers a profound message of comfort, reminding us that God’s providence extends to the minutest details of our lives. “Stand firm, and you will win life.” These words resonate as a call to resilience and unwavering faith. Jesus acknowledges that life may present us with trials and betrayals, even from those close to us. However, He empowers us to stand firm in our convictions and trust. We will not be defeated. His wisdom will guide our actions and decisions.
  - key: saint
    title: Saint of the Day
    saintName: "Blessed James Alberione, Priest Founder"
    ref: "Blessed James Alberione, Priest Founder"
    audio: null
    body: |
      Having finished high school at sixteen, frail James, fourth son of a devout peasant family, joined the seminary of Alba. On the midnight of 31 December 1900, the night that divided the two centuries, as he prayed, long and fervently, before the Blessed Sacrament in the Cathedral of Alba, a special light came from the monstrance and “he felt deeply obliged to prepare himself to do something for the Lord and for the people of the new century.” That “something” translated itself into the apostolate of the “good press” as he termed it, at a time when the Church looked upon the media hostilely in matters associated with the Gospel.



      Ordained priest in 1907, the Lord willed and guided James to use the media of social communications to spread the Gospel in the spirit of Paul the Apostle. In 1914, he laid the foundation of the *Society of St Paul* by opening a press training school in Alba. In 1915, he founded the *Daughters of St Paul* with the same mission. A man of vision, he started the Union of Lay Pauline Co-operators in 1917, giving lay involvement in the Pauline apostolate of the media due importance. Reading the signs of the times, he went on to found seven other Institutes, the ten collectively making up what is called the Pauline Family, the ideal of personal holiness and the mission of giving to the world ‘Jesus Master, the Way, the Truth and the Life’ being its source of unity and animation.



      He was beatified in 2003.
