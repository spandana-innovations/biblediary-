      Reflection: 1



      The world, that is, all those who reject God and His revelation, hates those who accept the revelation God gives in His Son Jesus Christ. He true disciple of Christ who lives a committed and responsible life meets with opposition and hatred. The forces of evil persecute the followers of Christ. Yet his disciples follow and imitate him. Therefore the enemies of Christ turn against them. If we are persecuted for being followers of Christ, we are in the company of the Lord, his martyrs and saints who were all persecuted. The Lord has predicted that the world which hated him will hate us his followers also, because they do not know that Jesus is sent by the Father. </span></span></span></span>



      *— Fr (Dr) George Kaitholil, SSP</span></span>*</span></span>



      Reflection: 2



      In today’s Gospel passage, taken from the Last Supper discourse, Jesus warns his apostles of what they can expect from a world which ignores God and His teaching. They will be hated and persecuted as Jesus was. But there can be no compromise between Christ’s disciples and the followers of the powers of darkness. The term “world” in today’s Gospel passage means people who are hostile towards God and opposed to His will. They represent an evil society which *“callsevil good and good evil”* (Is 5:20). Such a society will hate Christ and his teachings because Christian teaching exposes the evil of society and its false and dangerous doctrines. Since the Church Jesus established stands for truth, morality, and justice, it does not support the modern “dictatorship of relativism.” The modern world hates and ridicules everything Christian through its liberal, agnostic and atheistic media.



      Life message: Let us ask the Holy Spirit for the courage of our Christian convictions to believe and practice what Jesus taught and what Jesus continues to teach through the Church.



      *(Fr Tony: https://frtonyshomilies.com/)*

      The world hates Jesus because his presence demands some type of response involving belief or unbelief in him. This hatred of the world is directed towards the disciples of Jesus because when we try to follow and imitate him, we resemble him and are transformed into Christ in Holy Communion. This transformation and representation of Christ is painful for those who have “exchanged the truth of God for a lie” (Rom 1:25). When we experience hatred in the world, we are in the good company of Christ along with all the prophets and saints. As Christians, we are challenged not to conform ourselves to the world (Rom 12:2), but are called to change the culture of this world and bring about Christ’s Kingdom here and now.

      Pope Pius VII was imprisoned by Napoleon in 1808, but after the battle of Leipzig the 74-year-old Pontiff was once more set free, and in 1814 he returned to Rome in triumph. After Napoleon’s “Hundred Days” had been brought to an end in 1815 by the allied victory at Waterloo, Pius VII instituted today’s feast for the Papal States in thanksgiving to God and the Blessed Virgin, and to commemorate the anniversary of his return from Napoleonic captivity.



      The devotion to Mary under the title “Help of Christians” was actually fostered and propularized by Don Bosco who himself had a very deep devotion to Mary Help of Christians. He even erected a Basilica in her honour under that title in Turin in 1868 and later, in 1872, went on to found the Salesian Sisters under her patronage, viz. the Daughters of Mary Help of Christians).
