  - key: homily
    title: Homily Tip
    ref: "Mark 1:40-45"
    audio: null
    body: |
      The healing of the man with leprosy (Gospel) is just one instance of the mystery of salvation taking place through Jesus Christ, just one more illustration of God proving that He is mercy and love.



      The leper stands for the misery of man, from which Jesus came to lift him up: physical misery, but above all, spiritual misery. He stands for the sin-infected, disfigured humanity; he is the mirror of man’s soul without God. Jesus is the mirror of God’s heart of love and mercy. The leper is my soul, I am the leper. Jesus is my Saviour, and my salvation depends on my pleading, “If you will, you can make me clean.” And all that Jesus was, He is in the Eucharist.



      In the healing of the leper we see the divine compassion of the Lord for suffering humanity. Out of compassion and love for the human race He became man, fought the evil of suffering and sickness, and finally conquered it by His own suffering and death. In His infinite love for humankind, He placed heaven and a share in the life of God within our reach. We are always in need of His mercy because we are inclined to sin, and often we do sin.



      But He will heal us if we have the faith of the leper to plead: “If you will, you can make me clean,” faith in the mercy and power of Jesus. In the Gospel narrative He shows us that He does will, and does want to heal us, and His incarnation and death prove it beyond all doubt.



      (Philip John SSP & Premdas SSP, New Horizon Homilies, ST PAULS, Mumbai, India)
  - key: reflection
    title: Reflection
    ref: null
    audio: null
    body: |
      The words of the leper, “If you will, you can make me clean,” reflect his disposition and his openness to the power of God and the will of God. He knew he was with the Divine Healer, but he left the choice to Jesus as to whether to heal him or not. Jesus’ response “I will. Be clean” tells us that He desired the man’s healing and not only in word but by way of immediate action. Jesus gave back to the leper healthy skin, a sense of confidence and an increase of faith that propelled him to spread the word about what had taken place. From the ostracized and outcast position of the leper, Jesus raised his dignity and restored him body, soul, and spirit. What would I ask of Jesus if I met Him on the same road? Would I leave my life in His hands and trust that He knows best? Lord Jesus, save me from my present challenges and suffering if you choose to.
