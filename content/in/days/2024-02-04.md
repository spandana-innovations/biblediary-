  - key: homily
    title: Homily Tip
    ref: "Mark 1:29-39"
    audio: null
    body: |
      Brokenness is too pervasive to be elusive. How can humans face it? Confronted with it, the faith of many has fumbled and faltered. Unable to bear it, not a few have committed suicide.

      Here comes the Good News: The Lord heals the broken-hearted. This must have come from personal experience, for the psalmist is no stranger to suffering, betrayal and despair.



      In today’s Gospel Jesus acts cogently and coherently, vindicating the age-old claims made by and for God. In curing the sick, comforting the sorrowful and freeing the possessed, Jesus is healing the broken-hearted. Thus Jesus is what God has said He is and He shows what He will do. Jesus thus mirrors unmistakably His Father’s goodness and love in the murky areas of our life. In order to be His Father’s perfect image and to mirror Him clearly to men, Jesus has to be in communion with His Father. Thus we find Him, a great while before day, alone at prayer (Gospel). To be all to all (second reading) Jesus has to be first with the Father of all, the source of all goodness, love and comfort. In solidarity with humans, Jesus is not merely the healer, He is the broken, wounded healer. With His stripes we have been healed. This is the Good News, this is Jesus in person and action.



      The principal mission of every Christian is the mission of Jesus Christ, whose followers they are. What was Jesus’ mission? His mission was to proclaim the good news of salvation and to work out man’s salvation (Lk 4:18-19). “The time is fulfilled, and the kingdom of God is at hand; repent and believe in the Gospel” (Mk 1:15). “God sent the Son into the world, not to condemn the world, but that the world might be saved through Him” (Jn 3:17). Jesus accomplished His mission both by His words and deeds – preaching and working miracles. Today’s Gospel confirms it.



      (Philip John SSP & Premdas SSP, New Horizon Homilies, ST PAULS, Mumbai, India)

      As we reflect on today’s scripture passages, first we are confronted with the sense of zeal with which Saint Paul tirelessly delivered the saving message of the Gospel. The love of God prompted him to equally serve the weak, the strong, the rich, the poor, the Jews and the Gentiles alike. In the Gospel, we have before us a beautiful glimpse of Jesus’ servant-heart. His true love for people as He preached to them was demonstrated with signs and wonders. Not only did Jesus rise to preach the message of salvation but when the sick and the demon-possessed were brought to him, He brought the love of God to them through powerful healing and deliverance. However, we always see Jesus deep in communion with His Father before He went out on His mission. Lord Jesus, help me to serve you with generosity every day.
