<span class="rubric">The Priest begins the Eucharistic Prayer with the Preface Dialogue.</span>

<span class="celebrant">The Lord be with you.</span>

**And with your spirit.**

<span class="celebrant">Lift up your hearts.</span>

**We lift them up to the Lord.**

<span class="celebrant">Let us give thanks to the Lord our God.</span>

**It is right and just.**

<span class="rubric">At the end of the Preface, all sing or say the Sanctus.</span>

**Holy, Holy, Holy Lord God of hosts. Heaven and earth are full of your glory. Hosanna in the highest. Blessed is he who comes in the name of the Lord. Hosanna in the highest.**

<span class="rubric">After the Consecration, the Priest says:</span>

<span class="celebrant">The mystery of faith.</span>

**We proclaim your Death, O Lord, and profess your Resurrection until you come again.**
