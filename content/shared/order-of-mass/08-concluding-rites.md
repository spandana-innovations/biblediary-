<span class="rubric">Any brief announcements are made. Then the Blessing.</span>

<span class="celebrant">The Lord be with you.</span>

**And with your spirit.**

<span class="celebrant">May almighty God bless you, the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit.</span>

**Amen.**

<span class="rubric">The Deacon or the Priest dismisses the people.</span>

<span class="celebrant">Go forth, the Mass is ended.</span>

**Thanks be to God.**
