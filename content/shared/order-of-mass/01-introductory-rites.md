<span class="rubric">When the people are gathered, the Priest approaches the altar. All make the Sign of the Cross.</span>

<span class="celebrant">In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit.</span>

**Amen.**

<span class="celebrant">The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, and the love of God, and the communion of the Holy Spirit be with you all.</span>

**And with your spirit.**

<span class="rubric">The Priest invites the faithful to the Penitential Act.</span>

**I confess to almighty God and to you, my brothers and sisters, that I have greatly sinned, in my thoughts and in my words, in what I have done and in what I have failed to do.**

<span class="celebrant">May almighty God have mercy on us, forgive us our sins, and bring us to everlasting life.</span>

**Amen.**
