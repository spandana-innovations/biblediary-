<span class="rubric">The reader proclaims the first reading. At the end:</span>

<span class="celebrant">The word of the Lord.</span>

**Thanks be to God.**

<span class="rubric">The responsorial psalm follows, then the second reading and the Gospel Acclamation. The Deacon or Priest proclaims the Gospel.</span>

<span class="celebrant">The Lord be with you.</span>

**And with your spirit.**

<span class="celebrant">A reading from the holy Gospel according to N.</span>

**Glory to you, O Lord.**

<span class="rubric">At the end of the Gospel:</span>

<span class="celebrant">The Gospel of the Lord.</span>

**Praise to you, Lord Jesus Christ.**
