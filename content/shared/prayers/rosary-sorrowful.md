*Prayed on Tuesdays and Fridays.*

**1. The Agony in the Garden** — Jesus prays in Gethsemane. *Fruit of the mystery: sorrow for sin.*

**2. The Scourging at the Pillar** — Jesus is scourged. *Fruit of the mystery: purity.*

**3. The Crowning with Thorns** — Jesus is crowned with thorns. *Fruit of the mystery: courage.*

**4. The Carrying of the Cross** — Jesus carries his cross to Calvary. *Fruit of the mystery: patience.*

**5. The Crucifixion** — Jesus dies on the cross. *Fruit of the mystery: perseverance.*
