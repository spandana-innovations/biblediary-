*Prayed on Thursdays.*

**1. The Baptism in the Jordan** — The Father's voice is heard. *Fruit of the mystery: openness to the Spirit.*

**2. The Wedding at Cana** — Jesus works his first sign. *Fruit of the mystery: to Jesus through Mary.*

**3. The Proclamation of the Kingdom** — Jesus calls all to conversion. *Fruit of the mystery: repentance and trust.*

**4. The Transfiguration** — Jesus is transfigured on the mountain. *Fruit of the mystery: desire for holiness.*

**5. The Institution of the Eucharist** — Jesus gives his Body and Blood. *Fruit of the mystery: adoration.*
