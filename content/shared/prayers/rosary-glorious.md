*Prayed on Wednesdays and Sundays.*

**1. The Resurrection** — Jesus rises from the dead. *Fruit of the mystery: faith.*

**2. The Ascension** — Jesus ascends into heaven. *Fruit of the mystery: hope.*

**3. The Descent of the Holy Spirit** — The Spirit comes upon the Apostles. *Fruit of the mystery: love of God.*

**4. The Assumption** — Mary is assumed into heaven. *Fruit of the mystery: grace of a happy death.*

**5. The Coronation** — Mary is crowned Queen of Heaven. *Fruit of the mystery: trust in Mary's intercession.*
