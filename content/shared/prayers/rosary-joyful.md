*Prayed on Mondays and Saturdays.*

**1. The Annunciation** — The angel Gabriel announces to Mary that she is to be the Mother of God. *Fruit of the mystery: humility.*

**2. The Visitation** — Mary visits her cousin Elizabeth. *Fruit of the mystery: love of neighbour.*

**3. The Nativity** — Jesus is born in Bethlehem. *Fruit of the mystery: poverty of spirit.*

**4. The Presentation** — Jesus is presented in the Temple. *Fruit of the mystery: obedience.*

**5. The Finding in the Temple** — Jesus is found among the teachers. *Fruit of the mystery: joy in finding Christ.*
