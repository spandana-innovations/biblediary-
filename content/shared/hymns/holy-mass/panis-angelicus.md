Panis angelicus fit panis hominum;
Dat panis cælicus figuris terminum;
O res mirabilis! Manducat Dominum
Pauper, servus et humilis.

*The Bread of Angels becomes the Bread of mankind;
the Bread of Heaven puts an end to all foreshadowings.
O wondrous gift! The poor, the servant, and the lowly
feed upon their Lord.*
